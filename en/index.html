05 / Principles

Three tests for a good tool

Familiarity over novelty. Direct control over oversized systems. Software should earn its place in your daily rhythm.

01

Respect the rhythm

Keyboard and direct manipulation come first, keeping the route between intention and result short.

02

Stay local

Files and input stay under your control. Local processing is preferred whenever it makes sense.

03

Make it inspectable

Settings, logs, and source remain open so that people can understand the behavior and make informed choices.
